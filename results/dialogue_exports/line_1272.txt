3. **Increase in Thermal Energy**:
   The increase in thermal energy is the sum of the work done by the applied force and the absolute value of the change in kinetic energy:
   \[
   \Delta E_{\text{th}} = W_{\text{applied}} + |\Delta KE| = 20 \, \text{J} + 2.24 \, \text{J} = 22.24 \, \text{J}
