   \[
   W = F \times d = 40\, \text{N} \times 0.50\, \text{m} = 20\, \text{J}
   \]

   \[
   KE_{\text{initial}} = 0.5 \times 14\, \text{kg} \times (0.60\, \text{m/s})^2 = 2.52\, \text{J}
   \]

   \[
   KE_{\text{final}} = 0.5 \times 14\, \text{kg} \times (0.20\, \text{m/s})^2 = 0.28\, \text{J}
   \]

   \[
   \Delta KE = KE_{\text{final}} - KE_{\text{initial}} = 0.28\, \text{J} - 2.52\, \text{J} = -2.24\, \text{J}
   \]

5. **Thermal Energy Increase (ΔE_th):**
   \[
   \Delta E_{\text{th}} = W + | \Delta KE | = 20\, \text{J} + 2.24\, \text{J} = 22.24\, \text{J}
