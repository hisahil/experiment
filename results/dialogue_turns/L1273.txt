1. **Work Done by the Applied Force (W):**
   \[
   W = F \cdot d = 40 \, \text{N} \cdot 0.50 \, \text{m} = 20 \, \text{J}
   \]

2. **Initial Kinetic Energy (KE_initial):**
   \[
   KE_{\text{initial}} = \frac{1}{2} m v_0^2 = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 14 \, \text{kg} \cdot (0.60 \, \text{m/s})^2 = 2.52 \, \text{J}
   \]

3. **Final Kinetic Energy (KE_final):**
   \[
   KE_{\text{final}} = \frac{1}{2} m v^2 = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 14 \, \text{kg} \cdot (0.20 \, \text{m/s})^2 = 0.28 \, \text{J}
   \]

4. **Change in Kinetic Energy (ΔKE):**
   \[
   \Delta KE = KE_{\text{final}} - KE_{\text{initial}} = 0.28 \, \text{J} - 2.52 \, \text{J} = -2.24 \, \text{J}
   \]

5. **Increase in Thermal Energy (ΔE_th):**
   \[
   W + \Delta KE = \Delta E_{\text{th}}
   \]
   \[
   20 \, \text{J} + (-2.24 \, \text{J}) = \Delta E_{\text{th}}
   \]
   \[
   \Delta E_{\text{th}} = 22.24 \, \text{J}
   \]
